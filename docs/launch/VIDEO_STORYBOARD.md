## Direction

Use hard cuts and short 160-220 ms card glides. No fast zooms. Keep each meaningful line on screen for at least 1.5 seconds. The story must work without sound. Do not autoplay it on the website.

Record the current ShakeBoard app on a clean desktop with a fresh `SHAKEBOARD_DATA_DIR`. Do not paste into a real document. Use a blank temporary TextEdit file with fake content. Do not show notifications, personal menu-bar items, account names, real files, real clipboard history, or real paths.

The MCP frame must show consent without clipboard content. Redaction is best effort, so do not imply that it prevents every secret from being returned. Built-in signed updates shipped in 1.0 and are opt-in. Homebrew and encrypted backup and sync remain **Coming**.

Choose a poster frame from the ordered-selection shot around 0:18. It should make sense without motion and include useful alt text where published.
